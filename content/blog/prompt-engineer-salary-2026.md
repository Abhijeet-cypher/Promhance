# Prompt Engineer Salary in 2026: What the Data Really Shows (and What It Doesn't)

**By the Promhance Team** | **Published:** November 13, 2026 | **Last updated:** November 13, 2026 | **Reading time:** 9 min

## Quick answer

**Reported US figures for the "Prompt Engineer" title cluster around $113,000 to $138,000**, with Indeed's advertised average at $113,492 and Glassdoor's median reported at $126,000 in February 2026. Broader **AI Engineer** titles report higher, around $141,000 to $154,000 median base. The catch is that the dedicated title is rare, the samples behind many figures are small, and sources disagree widely. This guide shows the numbers, where each comes from, and how much weight to give them.

**Key takeaways**

- Title-specific salary data is thin. Indeed's figure rests on 49 postings over 36 months.
- Dedicated Prompt Engineer jobs are a small slice of AI hiring: under 0.5 percent of postings in one analysis of 20,662 LinkedIn listings.
- Prompting skills increasingly live inside broader titles like AI Engineer and Applied AI Specialist.
- Headline claims of huge growth or $200,000-plus pay are often hard to trace to primary data.
- All figures here are US-based and in US dollars. We found no reliable India-specific data.

## How much does a prompt engineer make in 2026?

There is no single number. Different sources measure different things (advertised base pay, self-reported pay, editorial estimates), different titles and different sample sizes. Here are the data points we could find, with our read on each.

| Source | Reported figure | Sample or date | Our read |
|---|---|---|---|
| Indeed, via [Simplilearn](https://www.simplilearn.com/career-roadmap/prompt-engineer-usa) | $113,492 average advertised base, "Prompt Engineer" title | 49 postings over 36 months, updated Sep 27, 2026 | Primary-source figure, but a small sample |
| Glassdoor, via [DEV Community](https://dev.to/tyson_cung/prompt-engineering-pays-126k-and-you-dont-need-to-write-a-single-line-of-code-1bab) | $126,000 median, $164,470 at the 75th percentile | February 2026 | Secondhand report, sample size not stated |
| Career guide, [Dupple](https://dupple.com/learn/how-to-become-an-ai-prompt-engineer) | $138,000 average, $200,000-plus for top earners | Updated March 2026 | Editorial estimate, method not stated |
| Glassdoor "AI Engineer," via [Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/blog/is-prompt-engineering-dead-what-replaced-it-and-what-still-pays-128k) | $141,172 median base | 858 salaries, March 2026 | Different title, larger sample |
| Levels.fyi "AI Engineer," same source | $154,000 median base, $280,000-plus median total compensation at FAANG-tier employers | 2026 | Different title, top-tier employers, total pay is not base pay |

**What this means.** If you search by the exact title, expect a reported range of roughly $113,000 to $138,000 in the US. If you widen to AI Engineer, the reported medians move up, but you are describing a different, more technical job.

JobRise also notes that at entry level the job may not even carry the Prompt Engineer title.

## How many prompt engineer jobs are there really?

Fewer than the headlines suggest, and the count depends entirely on how you measure.

- **Keyword search:** Simplilearn reported more than 9,000 US LinkedIn listings matching "Prompt Engineer" on October 5, 2026, but flagged it as a keyword match. Only 13 of the first 25 results had "Prompt" in the title.
- **Exact title, early 2026:** About 82 active US "Prompt Engineer" postings at a time, per an estimate from [AI Agents Kit](https://aiagentskit.com/blog/become-ai-prompt-engineer/).
- **Exact title, June 2026:** Roughly 200 to 400 open positions globally, per [AI-Pro](https://ai-pro.org/prompt-engineering-real-career).
- **Research sample:** One paper that analyzed 20,662 LinkedIn postings found only 72 prompt engineer roles, under 0.5 percent of the sample, as summarized by [Chat AI Guide](https://chatai.guide/?p=511).

These numbers are not directly comparable, since they cover different dates, regions and counting methods. The consistent message is that the exact title is niche.

## Why do prompt engineer salary sources disagree?

Four reasons explain most of the gap:

1. **Exact title vs. keyword match.** A search that catches "AI Engineer, prompt design experience preferred" inflates counts.
2. **Tiny samples.** An average from 49 postings can swing a lot with a few outliers.
3. **Old outliers.** AI-Pro notes that 2023 postings advertised salaries up to $375,000, a figure that still circulates but does not reflect 2026 hiring.
4. **Aggregator incentives.** Many salary guides are marketing pages for courses and rarely publish their method.

Some sites also report very large figures, such as hundreds of thousands of new openings in a quarter or triple-digit growth rates. We could not trace these to primary data, so we did not use them. That is not proof they are wrong, only a reason to be careful.

**A five-point check before you trust any salary claim:**

- Does it name the exact job title, or a loose group?
- How many postings or salary reports sit behind it?
- Is it base pay or total compensation?
- What country, and what date?
- Does it link to its data, or only repeat other sites?

## What replaced the prompt engineer title?

The skill did not disappear. It moved into broader roles. [AI Agents Kit](https://aiagentskit.com/blog/become-ai-prompt-engineer/) says prompt competency is increasingly embedded in titles like AI Engineer, Applied AI Specialist, LLM Product Engineer and AI Automation Specialist, and that organizations are training product managers in it. [JobRise](https://jobrise.io/en/blog/prompt-engineer-salary-2026-companies/) lists similar alternatives, including AI Product Lead, LLM Engineer, Applied AI Lead and AI Solutions Architect.

On the demand side, Let's Data Science reports that LinkedIn's Jobs on the Rise 2026 ranks AI Engineer as the fastest-growing role in the United States, with LangChain, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and PyTorch among its top skills. We have not verified that report directly, so treat that as secondhand.

| If you are... | What the data suggests |
|---|---|
| A job seeker | Search for AI-adjacent titles, not only "Prompt Engineer." Pair prompting with domain knowledge or technical skills. |
| A hiring manager | Budget against AI Engineer or applied AI benchmarks, not the narrow title. |
| A freelancer | Sell outcomes (workflows, evaluation, content systems), since the title carries little pricing power. |
| Just curious | Treat prompting as a core skill, like spreadsheets, rather than a standalone career bet. |

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a prompt engineer make in 2026?

In US data, figures reported for the Prompt Engineer title cluster between roughly $113,000 and $138,000. Indeed's average advertised base salary is $113,492 (49 postings over 36 months), Glassdoor's median was reported at $126,000 in February 2026, and one career guide cites $138,000 as an average. Broader AI Engineer titles report higher: a $141,172 Glassdoor median base and a $154,000 Levels.fyi median base.

### Is prompt engineer a real job title in 2026?

Yes, but a small one. Exact-title postings exist, yet estimates put them in the low hundreds at any time, and one analysis of 20,662 LinkedIn postings found only 72 prompt engineer roles, under 0.5 percent. Prompt skills now appear inside titles such as AI Engineer, Applied AI Specialist, LLM Product Engineer and AI Automation Specialist.

### How many prompt engineer jobs are there?

It depends on how you count. A keyword search on LinkedIn returned more than 9,000 US listings on October 5, 2026, but only 13 of the first 25 had Prompt in the title. Exact-title counts reported for 2026 are far lower: about 82 active US postings in one early-2026 estimate and roughly 200 to 400 globally in a June 2026 estimate.

### What is the salary range by experience level?

Career guides estimate about $70,000 to $95,000 for entry level, $110,000 to $160,000 for mid level and $160,000 to $205,000 for senior roles. These are editorial estimates, not primary survey data, so treat them as rough ranges.

### Do roles that need RAG or LangChain skills pay more?

One career guide reports a 15 to 25 percent premium for roles requiring RAG experience. LinkedIn's Jobs on the Rise 2026 report, as summarized by Let's Data Science, lists LangChain, RAG and PyTorch among the top skills for AI Engineer roles. Treat the premium as a reported estimate rather than a guarantee.

### Do I need to code to become a prompt engineer?

Pure prompt-focused roles lean on writing, analysis and domain knowledge, while the higher-paying AI Engineer titles list technical skills such as LangChain, RAG and PyTorch. If you want the higher end of the pay data, plan to add technical skills.

### What is the prompt engineer salary in India?

This article uses US data, and we did not find reliable India-specific salary figures while researching it, so we do not quote any. Check current Indian job boards and salary sites, and compare by exact job title, sample size and whether the figure is base pay or total compensation.

### Should I become a prompt engineer in 2026?

Build the skill rather than chase the title. Dedicated roles are rare, and the demand shows up inside broader AI and domain roles. Prompting combined with domain knowledge, evaluation habits and some technical depth is a stronger position than the title alone.
